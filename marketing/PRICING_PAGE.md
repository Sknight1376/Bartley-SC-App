## Pricing page headline

**Simple pricing for sailing clubs**

Choose a plan that fits your club size and launch stage. All plans are designed around club use, not per-sailor complexity.

**CTA:** Book a demo

---

## Pricing philosophy

We recommend charging per club, annually.

Why this works:
- easier for committees to approve
- simple to budget
- predictable support and renewal model
- avoids friction around counting individual sailors

## FAQ copy

### Do you charge per sailor?
No. We recommend club-based pricing so your committee can budget clearly without worrying about changing member counts.

### Is there a pilot option?
Yes. A limited pilot is the best way to validate fit with a small number of clubs before a wider rollout.

### Do you offer onboarding help?
Yes. We can support setup, training, and early race-day readiness.

### Can pricing change later?
Yes. As the product matures, you can refine plans based on club size, support level, and additional modules.

---

## Recommended commercial approach

For the first wave of clubs:
- offer 3 to 5 pilot clubs
- convert successful pilots to annual plans
- keep pricing simple and negotiable early on
- move to clearer standard pricing once testimonials and case studies are in place
