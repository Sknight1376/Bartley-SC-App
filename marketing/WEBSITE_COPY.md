## Home page

### Hero

**ClubHub for race day. SailorHub for every sailor.**

SailingHub helps clubs manage races, results, duties, and sailor access in one place — with ClubHub on the web for admins and SailorHub on mobile for sailors.

**Primary CTA:** Book a demo  
**Secondary CTA:** Join the pilot

### Trust strip

Built for real club operations:
- race-day admin
- results publishing
- sailor self-service
- mobile access for members

### Problem section

**Running club racing should not mean juggling spreadsheets, paper notes, and scattered messages.**

SailingHub brings the core race-day workflow together so clubs can spend less time on administration and more time on sailing.

### Solution section

**One connected system for club admins and sailors**

With SailingHub, clubs can:
- manage races and entries from ClubHub on the web
- publish results clearly
- support sailors with SailorHub access to races, duties, and results
- reduce confusion on busy race days

### Feature highlights

#### Club dashboard
Set up races, review activity, manage duties, and keep the day running smoothly.

#### Results and series
Publish results quickly and keep sailors informed with a clear view of standings and recent racing.

#### Mobile sailor experience
Give members access to upcoming races, latest results, and their sailing information from their phone.

#### Built for real race-day use
Designed around the practical needs of sailing clubs, volunteer race officers, and everyday members.

### Who it is for

**Perfect for:**
- sailing clubs
- race officers
- sailing secretaries
- volunteer admin teams
- clubs looking to modernise race-day operations
